## How we work

1. **Free consultation.** Tell us about your idea or problem. A complete brief is not required.
2. **Written quotation.** Deliverables, cost, and schedule are agreed before any work starts.
3. **Build on localhost.** We share progress as screenshots and videos while the project takes shape.
4. **Live preview.** We deploy to our own test server so you can use the real thing.
5. **Payment and deployment.** Once you are satisfied and have paid, we publish to your server and fix environment-related issues.
6. **Testing period.** You test everything, and we fix issues found in the agreed scope.

## Principles

- **Clear scope first.** Everything important is agreed in writing before work begins.
- **Progress you can see.** Real screenshots, videos, and live previews, not status reports.
- **Review before payment.** You approve the real work before you pay and before it goes live.
- **Finished means finished.** Deployment, environment fixes, and a testing period are part of the job.
